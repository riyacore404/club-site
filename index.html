What is Vividhata all about?

Vividhata is a dynamic community of passionate technocrats from diverse engineering backgrounds who come together to innovate, collaborate and grow.

How can I join the club?

Recruitment drives are announced through our official channels. Interested students can apply and shortlisted candidates continue through the selection process.

What events does the club organize?

We organize coding contests, workshops, technical seminars, cultural events, gaming events and many more events throughout the year.

Our events give students opportunities to learn from peers, network and meet like-minded people on a similar journey in technology.
